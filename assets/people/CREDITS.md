# Human operator

`operator.glb` is the `suited.glb` MakeHuman / MPFB 2 character distributed by
https://github.com/kunalkushwaha/vsim/tree/main/packages/assets/library

Source download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kunalkushwaha/vsim/main/packages/assets/library/suited.glb

The source manifest identifies the anatomy, skin and fitted clothes from the
MakeHuman system assets pack as CC0. Source animations are bundled in that file.
Manifest: https://github.com/kunalkushwaha/vsim/blob/main/packages/assets/library/manifest.json
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Magazzino Lab adapts garment materials to workwear, adds a head-bone-attached
helmet, samples the idle pose and controls a distance-driven gait and bounded
workstation gestures in the character's local coordinate system at runtime.
No external asset service is contacted by the application.
